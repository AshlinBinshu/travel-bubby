## Key Question 1: US policy under Trump in 2026 — tariffs, the India trade deal, the Supreme Court ruling, H-1B and immigration, US–China

### Takeaway
2026 was the year the US tariff regime was rebuilt by the courts: the Supreme Court struck down IEEPA tariffs on 20 Feb 2026, the administration switched to a 10% Section 122 surcharge (expired 24 July) and then Section 301 duties. India signed an interim trade framework on 6 Feb 2026 (US tariff on India cut to 18%, Russian-oil penalty tariff removed), but the full deal remained unsigned as of October. H-1B became wage-weighted, a $100,000 fee applies to overseas hires, and Indian consulates have effectively no H-1B appointment slots for the rest of 2026.

### Cited Findings

**India–US interim trade deal (Feb 2026)**
- A "framework for an Interim Agreement regarding reciprocal and mutually beneficial trade" was announced 6 Feb 2026; US reciprocal tariff on India dropped from 25% to 18%, and the additional 25% tariff imposed over India's Russian-oil purchases was removed by executive order — [White House joint statement](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/02/united-states-india-joint-statement/); [India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/us-india-interim-trade-agreement-18-percent-tariff-42514.html/)
- India committed to eliminating/reducing tariffs on US industrial and agricultural goods and to purchasing over $500 billion of US energy, technology and other products; Trump claimed India committed to zero tariffs, which India has not publicly confirmed — [Stimson Center](https://www.stimson.org/2026/implications-of-us-india-trade-announcements/); [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/india-us-trade-deal-2026)
- Commerce Minister Goyal said tariffs on Indian exports to the US cut to 18%; spices, tea, coffee, cashew and several fruits to attract zero US duty — [News on AIR, 7 Feb 2026](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/india-us-trade-deal-cuts-tariff-on-indian-exports-to-18-piyush-goyal)
- As of early Oct 2026 the broader deal remains unsigned; Goyal and USTR Jamieson Greer still finalising terms; complicated by a new US law allowing up to 100% tariffs on countries buying Russian oil — [GTsetu](https://gtsetu.com/fta/us-india-trade-deal/)
- 25 Feb 2026: US Commerce announced preliminary countervailing duties of ~126% on Indian crystalline silicon PV cells and modules — [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/us-tariff-on-india)
- Conflicting: Trading Economics reports a temporary 10% tariff on Indian goods after the Supreme Court ruling — [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/india/balance-of-trade/news/494916); a ClearTax page dated July 2026 still says 50% — [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/us-tariff-on-india). The two cannot both be right; the 18%/10% framing is better supported.

**Supreme Court and the tariff regime**
- 20 Feb 2026: Supreme Court in *Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump* held IEEPA does not authorise the president to impose tariffs — [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/02/supreme-court-strikes-down-ieepa-tariffs); [Global Trade Alert](https://globaltradealert.org/blog/from-ieepa-to-section-122)
- Replacement: Section 122 10% tariff on most imports from all countries effective 24 Feb 2026, capped at 150 days — [BDO](https://www.bdo.com/insights/tax/supreme-court-invalidates-ieepa-tariffs-administration-replaces-with-new-surcharge-what-importers); one source says Trump posted he was raising it to 15% (unconfirmed) — [Zonos](https://zonos.com/ieepa-tariff-ruling-updates)
- 7 May 2026: Court of International Trade held Section 122 tariffs unlawful (narrow relief, stayed on appeal) — [Skadden](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/05/us-trade-court-strikes-down-section-122-tariffs)
- Section 122 tariffs expired 24 July 2026 and were largely replaced by Section 301 "forced labor" tariffs of 10% or 12.5% on imports from 60 major economies; IEEPA refunds available via an automated system — [ABA](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/public_contract_law/resources/procurement-lawyer/2026-fall/ieepa-tariffs-struck-down-replacement-tariffs-expired/)

**Russian-oil sanctions law (Sept 2026)**
- The "Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026" was reported signed 18 Sept 2026, granting discretionary authority for tariffs up to 100% on countries importing Russian crude/gas; House vote 262-159 — [Whalesbook](https://www.whalesbook.com/news/English/economy/India-Pushes-for-Energy-Security-Clarity-After-US-Russia-Sanctions-Law/6ac1d5345aacb956d08ac7fb); [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/how-india-and-its-oil-economy-would-be-impacted-as-us-approves-sanctions-bill-on-russia)
- India's FY2026 Russian crude imports estimated at $40.8 bn (~one-third of crude imports); Russia's share of India's crude import value rose from 19.3% in Jan to 48.6% in June 2026 — [ChemAnalyst](https://www.chemanalyst.com/NewsAndDeals/NewsDetails/russian-crude-nears-50-of-indias-imports-will-us-tariffs-44556)
- Indian Ambassador Vinay Kwatra met US officials on energy security after the law was enacted — [Whalesbook](https://www.whalesbook.com/news/English/economy/India-Pushes-for-Energy-Security-Clarity-After-US-Russia-Sanctions-Law/6ac1d5345aacb956d08ac7fb)

**H-1B and immigration**
- DHS final rule (Dec 2025) created a wage-weighted H-1B selection; effective 27 Feb 2026, applies from the FY2027 cap season — [News on AIR, 26 Dec 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/us-h-1b-visa-selection-shifts-from-lottery-to-wage-based-system); [Karan Gupta](https://www.karangupta.com/blog/h1b-visa-changes-impact-indian-students)

- US ended automatic extension of work permits (EADs), hitting H-4 spouses — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/us-ends-automatic-extension-of-work-permits-for-foreigners/amp)
- India Ratings estimates H-1B changes trim remittances by up to $5 bn in FY26 (base case ~$2.8 bn, 0.07% of GDP) — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/h-1b-visa-changes-may-trim-indias-remittances-by-up-to-5-billion-in-fy26-india-ratings-and-research-3759978)

**US–China**
- Xi state visit to Washington late Sept 2026; trade truce extended only two months to 10 Jan 2027 (many expected 6+ months); both sides cut tariffs on ~$30 bn of goods; agricultural working group; China to buy 200 Boeing planes; a US–China "Super Intelligence Dialogue" on AI risks established — [CNBC, 24 Sept](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/24/us-china-trade-truce-bessent-trump-xi.html); [CNBC takeaways, 25 Sept](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/25/trump-xi-summit-takeaways.html)

### Inferences
- The India panel angle: India got tariff relief (18%) earlier than most, but the unsigned full deal, the $500 bn purchase commitment, the Russian-oil sanctions law and the solar CVD give the US continuing leverage. Expect "Did India concede too much?" and "Can India keep buying Russian oil?" questions.
- The Supreme Court ruling means tariff policy now rests on narrower statutory tools (122/301/232), which makes US trade policy more legally contestable but not less protectionist.
- H-1B: the shift to wage-weighting plus consular backlog favours in-country F-1→H-1B transitions and GCC (offshoring) growth in India over fresh deputations.

## Key Question 3: Middle East — the 2026 Iran war, Hormuz, Gaza, Red Sea, and India's exposure

### Takeaway
The dominant global story of 2026: on 28 Feb 2026 the US and Israel struck Iran, killing Supreme Leader Khamenei; Iran closed the Strait of Hormuz; a Pakistan-brokered ceasefire (8 April) and a June memorandum collapsed in July; a US naval blockade was reimposed; Brent has traded ~$100–115 (spiking to $140 dated Brent); as of 8–9 Oct 2026 the Pentagon was preparing for possible resumed "major combat operations". India evacuated >52,000 from the Gulf in the first week of March and 2,557 overland from Iran; ~$51 bn of Gulf remittances and ~half of crude/three-quarters of LPG imports are exposed.

- 19 March: US air campaign to reopen the strait; pre-war ~25% of seaborne oil and 20% of LNG transited Hormuz; tanker transits fell near zero — [Wikipedia: Hormuz campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Strait_of_Hormuz_campaign)
- 8 April: two-week US–Iran ceasefire organised by Pakistan; Islamabad Talks failed; 13 April US naval blockade of Iranian ports (ran to mid-June) — [Wikipedia: ceasefire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war_ceasefire); [Wikipedia: blockade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_naval_blockade_of_Iran)
