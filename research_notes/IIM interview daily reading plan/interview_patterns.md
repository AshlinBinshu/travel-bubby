## Key Question 1: What proportion of PI questions are current-affairs based, and which specific news topics were asked (2024–2026)?

### Takeaway
No source gives a measured proportion; the best evidence is qualitative. Candidate transcripts from 2024–2026 show current affairs is rarely the *spine* of an IIM interview (the spine is work-experience/academics/"why MBA"), but it is a recurring *second layer* that panels switch to when they probe an answer — and at IIM-B and the newer/CAP IIMs it can dominate. Specific 2025 topics that appear in transcripts: Union Budget 2025 (new tax slabs, customs-duty cuts on medicines/EV batteries/semiconductors), sovereign bonds, RBI policy and the rupee, pharma/bank regulators, ChatGPT vs DeepSeek, globalisation, AI regulation, GST/PLI, SC judge appointments, constitutional amendments, fuel-price rises.

### Inferences
- Across the 2024–2026 transcripts found, the typical IIM-A/B/C/K interview is roughly: 1 opening personal question → 50–70% work-ex/academics → a current-affairs "pivot" triggered by something the candidate said (a term like "risk-free", "budget", "semiconductors", "globalisation") → 1–3 opinion/stance questions. Pure quiz-style GK ("who is the governor of X") shows up mostly at CAP/newer IIMs and in older (pre-2023) transcripts.
- The news topics that recur in 2025 transcripts are overwhelmingly *economic-policy* news (Budget, RBI, bonds, regulators, GST, tariffs) and *AI* news (DeepSeek, AI regulation, AI and jobs). Pure political or sports news appears far less often in the 2024–2026 material found.
- Because panels pivot off the candidate's own words, the "proportion" of current-affairs questions is partly under the candidate's control: naming a news item invites 2–4 follow-ups on that item.

### Gaps
- No source quantifies the share of current-affairs questions; the one percentage rubric (IIM-L 15% general awareness) is an unverified blog claim.
- Reddit r/CATpreparation and PaGaLGuY 2025/2026 threads did not surface in any search; the CAT-2025-cycle (2026 interviews) transcripts are especially thin — only prep guides dated 2026 and one unverified IIM-A AWT anecdote were found.
- Full InsideIIM transcript pages could not be opened, so only the questions quoted in snippets are captured; the pages likely contain more.

## Key Question 2: WAT/AWT topics actually given in 2024, 2025, 2026 at the top IIMs (verbatim where found)

### Takeaway
Confirmed, dated topics are scarce but consistent in style: IIM-A gives an *argument/case to critique* (AWT), IIM-B gives an argument-plus-recommendation prompt, IIM-C/K/L give opinion essays on social/tech/economy themes (AI, social media, women, DEI, waste, globalisation), and CAP IIMs give short policy/tech prompts (UPI, AI and jobs, Viksit Bharat 2047). IIM Indore dropped WAT from the 2026-28 batch and uses GD+PI; the JAP 2026 IIMs and CAP 2026 dropped WAT/GD entirely.

## Key Question 3: What do panels expect from "tell me three news items" / "what's the latest on X", and how deep do they go?

### Takeaway
Panels use the "any recent news?" question as a *launchpad*, not a quiz: they pick one item from the candidate's list (usually the business/economic one) and go 2–4 levels deep — numbers, government position, why it matters, the candidate's stance, and a factual tie-in (a capital city, a regulator's name, a fraud amount). The expected answer is a short list of items the candidate can explain end-to-end, with one defensible opinion.

### Cited Findings
- **IIM Rohtak:** "Also tell us two news you've seen in today's newspaper? (I told them about abg shipyard case, they asked me the value of fraud, answered)" — [GradSquare IIM CAP (new IIMs) interview](https://gradsqr.com/iim-cap-new-iims-interview/)
- **Selective follow-up:** "I told about the hijab protest, Russia-Ukraine conflict, and the freedom convoy in Canada. They ignored hijab and freedom convoy news and asked specifically about the Russia-Ukraine conflict." — [CATKing IIM Ahmedabad PI experiences](https://catking.in/iim-ahmedabad-personal-interview-experiences)
- **Trivia tie-in:** "What is the capital of Arunachal Pradesh? (A follow up question because I had mentioned PM's visit to Arunachal Pradesh)" — [CATKing IIM Bangalore PI experiences](https://catking.in/iim-bangalore-personal-interview-experiences)
- **Policy-angle follow-up:** "Semiconductors are trending as a news in news as the Govt.'s take on it, why do you think that is?" — the candidate was pushed to explain the government's position and then why the topic was trending — [CATKing IIM-A](https://catking.in/iim-ahmedabad-personal-interview-experiences)
- **"Do you read the news?" as a reproach (IIM-B 2025):** after the candidate couldn't discuss sovereign bonds, the panelist noted it "had been in every major paper", asked whether the candidate read the news, then "any recent news?" — [InsideIIM IIM-B 2025](https://insideiim.com/iim-bangalore-interview-experiences-2025)
- **Source-naming follow-up (IIM-A):** asked whether they read the news, a candidate answered that they follow the Economic Times and Moneycontrol — [CATKing IIM-A](https://catking.in/iim-ahmedabad-personal-interview-experiences)
- **Evidence demand:** panels "challenge it with direct follow-up — 'But why should legacy systems change? Give me specific evidence.'" — [Rehearsal: IIM Interview Experience 2026 inside look](https://tryrehearsal.ai/blog/iim-interview-experience-2026-inside-look)
- **Cue-driven questioning:** "the interviewers usually take cues from your answers to base their follow-up questions. So, it is important to answer each question carefully, anticipating the next question." — [InsideIIM: How to prepare for IIM interviews](https://insideiim.com/prepare-iim-interviews)
- **Admitting ignorance is acceptable:** one candidate asked about election news said they didn't know and the interview moved on — [CATKing IIM-A](https://catking.in/iim-ahmedabad-personal-interview-experiences); a CAT topper who converted IIM-B: "if I don't know any answer, it is OK to accept that I do not know" — [Careers360 Rishab Rahiman IIM-B](https://bschool.careers360.com/articles/cat-toppers-conversation-with-rishab-rahiman-converted-iim-bangalore)
- **Depth expectation from coaching:** "Don't just skim headlines. Follow three or four major issues in depth"; cover "the past three months, with extra attention to developments linked to your work experience or career interests" — [Collegedekho IIM interview documents & tips 2026](https://www.collegedekho.com/articles/important-documents-iim-interview-tips/); [InsideIIM prepare for IIM interviews](https://insideiim.com/prepare-iim-interviews)
- **Business-impact framing expected:** interviewers "want to know if you can connect big economic decisions to real-world business effects"; guides for 2025–26 say be ready to discuss "India's fiscal policies, actions taken by the Reserve Bank of India, or how recent budget announcements impact specific sectors" — [IIM Admit Mentors PI questions 2025-26](https://www.iimadmitmentors.com/post/iim-personal-interview-questions-2025-26)
- **Recovery is scored:** "Recovery from a poor answer matters more than any single answer — panels observe how you handle difficulty" — [Rehearsal 2026 inside look](https://tryrehearsal.ai/blog/iim-interview-experience-2026-inside-look)

### Inferences
- The depth ladder observed in transcripts: (1) name the item → (2) a fact/number (fraud value, tax slab, duty cut) → (3) the government/RBI/court position → (4) "why is this happening / why does it matter" → (5) "what is your view" + "give evidence". A daily-reading routine should capture levels 2–4 for each story, not just the headline.
- Panels prefer the economic/business item in a mixed list; leading with a business story steers the follow-ups into prepared territory.
- Fabricating depth is riskier than conceding ignorance; multiple accounts show a plain "I don't know" is survivable, while a wrong regulator name (DCGI) was exposed immediately.

### Gaps
- No panelist-side (faculty) statement of what a "good" news answer looks like was found; everything is candidate- or coach-reported.
- No 2026-cycle transcript of a "three news items" exchange was found.
