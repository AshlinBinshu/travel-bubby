# Thirty focused minutes a day converts IIM calls

A CAT 2026 candidate with 30 minutes a day should stop reading headlines and start reading **two articles properly plus a 3-minute explainer, then spend seven minutes writing a one-page note on one of three or four running issues**: that is the routine IIM converts and coaching mentors converge on, and it fits the evidence on what panels actually do with current affairs. The 2024–2026 interview transcripts show that panels rarely quiz general knowledge; they pivot off a word the candidate uses ("risk-free", "Budget", "semiconductors", "globalisation") and then climb a ladder of two to four follow-ups, from a number to the government's position to "what is your view, and what is your evidence" ([InsideIIM IIM-B 2025](https://insideiim.com/iim-bangalore-interview-experiences-2025); [CATKing IIM-A](https://catking.in/iim-ahmedabad-personal-interview-experiences)). The news that recurs in those transcripts is overwhelmingly economic policy (Budget, RBI, bonds, GST, tariffs, regulators) and AI, which maps onto the economy pages of a business daily plus the editorial page of a general daily, so the plan below uses **Finshots (free, 3 min), one editorial from The Hindu or Indian Express, one economy story from Mint, Economic Times or Business Standard, a running issue diary, and PRS India's free Monthly Policy Review once a month**, with a Zerodha Daily Brief or Hindu "In Focus" podcast as the commute substitute. The routine is phased: a bare 15-minute habit until CAT on **29 November 2026**, foundational economy reading and profile sheets in December, the Economic Survey, Budget and RBI numbers sheet plus alternate-day timed WATs from late December, and institute-specific mocks once shortlists arrive in early-to-mid January 2027 (2026-cycle proxy) ahead of interviews concentrated in February–March 2027. The must-know 2026 story is a single "connect the dots" chain: the US–Israel war on Iran from 28 February 2026 closed Hormuz, pushed Brent above $100, drove the rupee to ~Rs 96–97/$, forced a Rs 10/litre excise cut and record RBI dollar sales, and on 7 October 2026 produced the RBI's first rate hike since February 2023 (repo to 5.50%, "calibrated tightening") even as FY27 growth was raised to 7.1% ([Business Standard, 7 Oct 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/finance/news/rbi-mpc-raises-fy27-inflation-forecast-to-5-2-projects-gdp-growth-at-7-1-126100700283_1.html)). Everything in this report that rests on the 2026 admission cycle rather than a published 2027 date is labelled **[PROXY 2026]** or **[ESTIMATE]**.

## Panels pivot off your own words, then climb a four-rung ladder

The first thing the evidence settles is that current affairs is not the spine of an IIM interview; it is the second layer the panel reaches for when they want to test depth. At IIM Calcutta in February 2025 the interview was "mostly conversational, went into very depth of my work experience" before the panel asked what the candidate understood by globalisation and its positives and negatives ([InsideIIM IIM-C 2025](https://insideiim.com/iim-calcutta-interview-transcript)). At IIM Kozhikode in March 2025 a non-engineer with 2.5 years at Asian Paints was asked about the company's premium products, industrial coatings, turnover, overseas revenue and office count, with no general news at all ([InsideIIM IIM-K 2025](https://insideiim.com/iim-kozhikode-mba-personal-interview-transcripts)). At IIM Ahmedabad in 2024 one PI was "mostly finance and quantitative": CAPM, how beta is calculated, WACC, valuation methods, correlation versus regression ([InsideIIM 2024 roundup](https://insideiim.com/iim-interview-transcripts-from-abc-cap-colleges)). The pattern across 2024–2026 is roughly one opening personal question, 50–70% work experience and academics, a current-affairs pivot triggered by something the candidate said, and one to three opinion questions. Pure quiz-style GK shows up mostly at the CAP and newer IIMs, where a candidate was asked to name two news items from that day's paper and then, having named the ABG Shipyard case, the value of the fraud ([GradSquare IIM CAP](https://gradsqr.com/iim-cap-new-iims-interview/)).

The pivot mechanism is what makes a reading plan matter. In the most instructive 2025 transcript, an IIM Bangalore candidate mentioned "risk-free returns" during a discount-rate calculation; the second panelist cross-questioned until they reached sovereign bonds, and when the candidate admitted not having read about them, the panelist said the topic "had been in every major paper", asked whether the candidate read the news at all, and then demanded "any recent news?" ([InsideIIM IIM-B 2025](https://insideiim.com/iim-bangalore-interview-experiences-2025)). Another IIM-B candidate on the same page was asked about Union Budget reforms, answered "new tax slabs", and was pushed for two more, producing "custom duties removal in medicines and EV battery and semiconductor". A GMAT Club debrief from the same cycle reports "almost all the other questions asked was on my work ex, current affairs and multiple cross questioning", with a panel that "reacted harshly to answers they did not expect" ([GMAT Club IIM-B](https://gmatclub.com/forum/iim-bangalore-452/interview-debrief.html)). The ladder the transcripts reveal has four rungs: name the item; give a fact or number (fraud value, tax slab, duty cut); state the government's, RBI's or court's position; explain why it is happening and what you think, with evidence ([Rehearsal 2026 inside look](https://tryrehearsal.ai/blog/iim-interview-experience-2026-inside-look)). Panels choose which item to climb. A candidate who listed the hijab protests, the Russia–Ukraine war and Canada's freedom convoy found the IIM-A panel "ignored hijab and freedom convoy news and asked specifically about the Russia-Ukraine conflict" ([CATKing IIM-A](https://catking.in/iim-ahmedabad-personal-interview-experiences)); an IIM-B candidate who mentioned the PM's Arunachal visit was asked the capital of Arunachal Pradesh ([CATKing IIM-B](https://catking.in/iim-bangalore-personal-interview-experiences)). The practical consequence is that **the share of current-affairs questions is partly under your control**: lead with the business or economic item you have prepared end-to-end, because that is the one panels prefer to pick.

Two further findings shape the plan. First, admitting ignorance is survivable while fabricating is not: an IIM-B convert's advice is that "if I don't know any answer, it is OK to accept that I do not know" ([Careers360, Rishab Rahiman](https://bschool.careers360.com/articles/cat-toppers-conversation-with-rishab-rahiman-converted-iim-bangalore)), whereas a candidate who named DCGI as the pharma regulator was exposed on the follow-up because DCGI is a post, not a body ([Glassdoor IIM-B 2025](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/IIM-Bangalore-Interview-Questions-E377025.htm)). Second, news is tied to profile. Engineers get semiconductors and "why is the government's take on semiconductors trending" ([CATKing IIM-A](https://catking.in/iim-ahmedabad-personal-interview-experiences)); commerce and finance profiles get GST, CAPM/WACC and "why the RBI increased the repo rate" ([Btribe IIM-B 2025](https://www.btribe.in/blog/iim-bangalore-interview-questions-2025-real-questions-answer-frameworks-strategy)); work-ex candidates get their employer's competitors, sales strategy and sector trends such as EV adoption ([Hitbullseye IIM-B](https://gdpi.hitbullseye.com/IIM-bangalore.php)); and home-state questions, from the Kerala tourism sector after COVID to naming entrepreneurs from Bihar, cluster at IIM-K, IIM-I and the newer IIMs ([GradSquare IIM K/I/S](https://gradsqr.com/iim-k-i-s-interview-experiences/); [IIMKing new IIMs](https://iimking.com/new-and-baby-iim-interview-experience/)). IIM Bangalore makes employer depth structurally important by multiplying the work-experience score by a panel-assigned quality rating of 0.25 to 2 ([Quantifiers IIM-B 2026](https://quantifiers.in/iim-bangalore-selection-criteria-2026/)).

### WAT themes cluster around AI, society and gender; PI carries the weight

The confirmed, dated WAT and AWT topics of 2024–2025 are consistent in style and favour opinion over data. IIM Bangalore 2025: "A well renowned AI historian writes that AI can learn and change on its own, therefore it's hard to control. Comment about this argument and suggest ways to deal with such predictions" ([InsideIIM IIM-B 2025](https://insideiim.com/iim-bangalore-interview-experiences-2025)). IIM Calcutta, 19 February 2025: "Social media is creating a fake world... Discuss its negative impacts" ([InsideIIM IIM-C](https://insideiim.com/iim-calcutta-interview-transcript)). IIM Kozhikode, 3 March 2025: "Should a regulatory body be formed to oversee AI regulation?" and, in another slot, "In the 21st century, women are not equal to men but superior to them" ([InsideIIM IIM-K](https://insideiim.com/iim-kozhikode-mba-personal-interview-transcripts)). IIM Lucknow, 7 February 2025: "Should Sports, Spirituality, and Stress Management be part of the B-school curriculum?" ([InsideIIM IIML](https://insideiim.com/iim-lucknow-personal-interview-experiences)). CAP 2025: "Is UPI a risky convenience?", "Should AI be controlled to protect employment?" and "Pathways for Developed India @ 2047" ([InsideIIM CAP 2025](https://insideiim.com/cap-interview-experiences-2025); [MBAUniverse CAP](https://www.mbauniverse.com/new-iims-admission-process-cap)). IIM Ahmedabad's AWT is different in kind: it hands you an argument and asks you to find assumptions and argue both sides, and the panel then challenges your AWT claims in the PI ("why they claimed the author assumed agriculture wasn't part of development, and what proof they had") ([CATKing IIM-A](https://catking.in/iim-ahmedabad-personal-interview-experiences)). Theme frequency across confirmed topics runs AI, then social media and technology-in-society, then gender and DEI, then economy and policy, then abstract prompts; hard economic data appears far more in PI than in WAT.

The weights explain where daily reading pays. PI is the heaviest single component everywhere: **50% at IIM-A, 48% at IIM-C, 40% at IIM-B and IIM-L, 35% at IIM-K**, while WAT or AWT is 8–20% (IIM-C 8, IIM-A/B/L 10, IIM-K 20) ([InsideIIM IIM-A 2026-28](https://insideiim.com/iim-ahmedabad-selection-criteria-2026-28-cat-2025); [Cracku IIM-C 2027](https://cracku.in/iim-calcutta-selection-criteria-2027); [Collegedekho IIM-B](https://www.collegedekho.com/articles/iim-bangalore-selection-criteria/); [IIML policy 2026-28 PDF](https://www.iiml.ac.in/sites/default/files/upload/news/2143004504IIML%20Admission%20Policy%20Batch%202026-2028.pdf); [Careers360 IIM-K 2026](https://bschool.careers360.com/articles/iim-kozhikode-admission-criteria-2026-released-cat-2025-cutoff-selection-criteria-10-12-gender-work-ex--weightage-cs-score)). IIM-L excludes anyone scoring below 12/40 in PI from the merit list. For the 2027-29 batch, IIM-A's policy published around 7–8 October 2026 keeps PI at 50% and AWT at 10% but shortlists on a 65:35 CAT-percentile-to-application-rating composite with discipline-wise normalisation ([Percentyl IIMA 2027-29](https://percentyl.in/notices/iim-ahmedabad-releases-pgp-2027-29-admission-policy-cat-95-percentile-cut-off-65)); IIM-C has retained its 2026-28 process ([Cracku IIM-C 2027](https://cracku.in/iim-calcutta-selection-criteria-2027)); IIM-B, L, K and I policies for 2027-29 were not yet published as of early October 2026. The structural trend is the newer IIMs dropping written tests: IIM Indore removed WAT from the 2026-28 batch and uses GD plus PI ([Zollege IIM Indore](https://zollege.in/university/184986-iimi-indian-institute-of-management-indore/admission)), and the JAP 2026 IIMs (Raipur, Ranchi, Kashipur, Trichy) and CAP 2026 ran PI only ([Rehearsal IIM Raipur](https://www.tryrehearsal.ai/schools/iim-raipur)), which pushes all awareness testing into the interview. The marginal return of daily reading is therefore highest where it feeds PI follow-ups (economic policy, your sector, AI) and lowest for memorising essay templates, with IIM-K's 20% WAT the one place a strong essay moves the needle materially.

## The 30-minute routine: Finshots, two articles, one page

Every source that gives a time-bounded recommendation lands in the same place. An IIM Lucknow student's InsideIIM guide prescribes "two kinds of newspapers - general and business", preferring The Hindu and Mint, and says "2 quality articles per day would suffice", framing the purpose as "Opinions help you build arguments. Facts help you back up arguments" ([InsideIIM 3-step approach](https://insideiim.com/get-iim-tips-iim-lucknow-student)). An IIM Ahmedabad preparation guide suggests "30–45 mins daily with business news and summaries" ([Collegedunia IIM-A guide](https://collegedunia.com/news/c-25494-iim-ahmedabad-interview-preparation-guide)); IIM Shillong's 2024 preparation kit names Mint for finance plus the editorial sections of Indian Express and The Hindu ([IIM Shillong kit](https://www.scribd.com/document/705200085/Preparation-Kit-2024-IIM-Shillong)); Cracku's post-CAT guide says focus on "the National & Business section, and editorial columns" and make short notes you can use to build arguments ([Cracku post-CAT GD-PI-WAT](https://cracku.in/how-to-prepare-for-gd-pi-wat-after-cat-2025/)); and the IIM Indore IPM guide for 2026 says pick "3-4 major national or international issues and follow them deeply" ([ipmcareer IIM Indore 2026](https://register.ipmcareer.com/magazine/iim-indore-interview-strategy-2026-a-guide-for-future-iimers-5pbwg)). The 2025 questions actually recorded (Budget customs duties on semiconductors and EV batteries, sovereign bonds, RBI and the rupee, DeepSeek, AI regulation, globalisation, tariffs) are precisely the front-page, economy-page and edit-page content of ET or Business Standard and The Hindu; a plan built on one business daily's economy pages plus one general daily's editorial page would have covered every 2025 PI news question found. Mint is not credited by any transcript but covers the same beats, and an IIM-A candidate who was asked whether they read news answered Economic Times and Moneycontrol ([CATKing IIM-A](https://catking.in/iim-ahmedabad-personal-interview-experiences)).

The routine below is the synthesis. It is deliberately source-light so that it is sustainable for six months; starting a newspaper only after calls arrive is the approach one IIM-A/B convert explicitly calls "questionable" ([Debashish Reang](https://reangdeba.xyz/iimpi/)).

| Minutes | Activity | Named source | Why this and not something else |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Scan the day's one explainer | **Finshots** (free, one email a morning, self-described 3-minute read, 5 lakh+ readers) ([Finshots](https://cred.finshots.in/)) | The only widely cited daily explainer that is both free and short; gives you the "why it matters" framing panels want |
| 3–11 | Read one editorial or op-ed fully | **The Hindu** editorial page or **Indian Express** editorial/"Explained" page (Hindu is metered at 10 free articles a month ([WAN-IFRA](https://wan-ifra.org/2023/08/deep-dive-how-the-hindu-built-its-subscription-business/)); IE's meter is unpublished) | Builds the "for/against + stance" muscle that WAT and opinion probes test; IE Explained simplifies policy ([PW on IE Explained](https://www.pw.live/exams/upsc/best-newspaper-for-upsc-exam/)) |
| 11–20 | Read one economy or business story fully | **Mint** (Premium ~Rs 1,499/yr with 14-day trial ([GrabOn Mint](https://www.grabon.in/mint-coupons/))), **Business Standard** (Smart Annual Rs 1,999, Monthly Star Rs 249 per its own FAQ ([BS FAQ](https://bsppd.business-standard.com/subscription-cart/faq))) or **Economic Times** (ET Prime ~Rs 2,549/yr ([pricehistory ET Prime](https://pricehistory.app/p/et-prime-one-year-digital-subscription-economic-waLVqfQ2))) | This is where sovereign bonds, Budget duties, RBI and rupee questions come from; pick the RBI/Budget/trade/your-sector story over the markets story |
| 20–27 | Update one issue page in your diary | A paper notebook or a Notion page with fixed fields: topic, date, key facts and numbers, stakeholders, arguments for, arguments against, India/business link, your stance, likely follow-up | The two note methods attributed to IIM converts are a current-events diary ([InsideIIM, Bushra Qureshi](https://insideiim.com/how-to-convert-an-iim-interview-call)) and a notebook per issue with both sides and a chosen stance ([InsideIIM 3-step](https://insideiim.com/get-iim-tips-iim-lucknow-student)) |
| 27–30 | Rehearse aloud | Say the day's issue in 60 seconds the way you would to a panel: item, number, government position, your view | Panels "take cues from your answers to base their follow-up questions" ([InsideIIM prepare](https://insideiim.com/prepare-iim-interviews)); rehearsal makes the pivot land in prepared territory |

Three or four issue pages should run at any time, and the discipline is to keep each one current rather than to start a new page every day. On a weekend day, swap the two articles for a 20-minute read of **PRS Legislative Research's Monthly Policy Review**, a free, CC-BY-licensed digest of the month's policy developments that "does not provide recommendations or opinions" (the September 2026 edition appeared on 3 October) ([PRS Annual Policy Review](https://prsindia.org/policy/annual-policy-review)), or of a PIB summary of a major document such as the Economic Survey ([PIB Economic Survey 2025-26 backgrounder](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/jan/doc2026130774501.pdf)). On a commute day, the reading can be replaced by audio: **Zerodha's The Daily Brief** (~20–23 minutes, two main stories plus "tidbits", explicitly framed around Indian markets and the global business landscape) plus **Finshots Daily** (~7 minutes) fits a 30-minute commute exactly ([Zerodha Daily Brief](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/updates/the-daily-brief-podcast-markets-by-zerodha); [Podchaser Finshots Daily](https://www.podchaser.com/podcasts/finshots-daily-1461204)), as does **The Hindu's "In Focus"** (~28 minutes average) ([Listen Notes In Focus](https://www.listennotes.com/podcasts/in-focus-by-the-hindu-the-hindu-6Qu-YC4VWUH/)) or an Indian Express "3 Things" main episode plus a Catch Up bulletin ([Listen Notes 3 Things](https://www.listennotes.com/podcasts/3-things-express-audio-eRHgpxNdEFo/)). No coaching institute or convert recommends a podcast as a primary source, so audio is a substitute, not the plan.

Budget for the stack is between zero and about Rs 1,500 a year. The free tiers (Finshots, PRS, PIB, The Hindu's ten articles, Mint's and IE's unpublished meters) cover the two-articles-a-day pattern only if spread across papers; a single subscription of roughly Rs 1,000–1,500 removes the meter, with The Hindu's digital annual at ~Rs 999 in its 2026 sales ([Zoutons Hindu sale](https://zoutons.com/news/the-hindu-subscription-sale-flat-50-off-on-annual-plans)) and Indian Express's yearly digital around Rs 1,199 ([GrabOn IE](https://www.grabon.in/indian-express-coupons/)). Every price except Business Standard's comes from coupon aggregators and should be verified at checkout. Several things are worth skipping on the evidence. Inshorts is built on the premise that "the first paragraph of a news story usually contains enough" ([StartupTalky Inshorts](https://startuptalky.com/inshorts-startup-story/)), which is exactly what the four-rung ladder omits; use it, if at all, as a two-minute scan to choose which story to read in full. The Ken (Basic $75/yr list, Rs 5,999 on the app) and The Morning Context ($99/yr list) are long-form, expensive and not cited by any prep source ([The Ken pricing](https://the-ken.com/pricing/); [TMC pricing](https://themorningcontext.com/pricing)). Yojana, Kurukshetra and Frontline are UPSC staples that no IIM prep source recommends. Think School-style 20–30-minute business case videos consume the whole budget for one topic; the only video an IIM alumnus explicitly recommends is StudyIQ's analysis of The Hindu's editorials and The Quint's short clips ([InsideIIM IIM-L pre-interview](https://insideiim.com/iim-lucknow-decoding-the-iim-selection-process-pre-interview-preparation-1)).

## Phase the plan: habit now, foundations in December, numbers in January

The calendar sets the phasing, and it is tighter than most candidates assume. CAT 2026 is on **Sunday 29 November 2026** in three sessions with IIM Indore convening, registration having closed on 15 September; the admit card is reported for 4 November and the result is tentatively in the third week of December ([IMS CAT 2026 notification](https://www.imsindia.com/blog/cat/cat-exam-notification/); [Careers360 CAT 2026](https://bschool.careers360.com/articles/iim-cat-2026-latest-update-notification-on-sunday-iim-indore-november-29-exam-date-egistration-start-date)). The 2025 result came on **24 December 2025**, before New Year rather than in January ([Career Launcher IIM calls](https://www.careerlauncher.com/blogs/cat-mba/iims-calls-out/)), and the 2026-cycle shortlists followed within days: IIM-A and IIM-C on 8 January, IIM-B on 13 January, IIM-K on 16 January ([Careers360 shortlist 2026 live](https://bschool.careers360.com/articles/iim-shortlist-2026-live-pi-wat-calls-dates-cut-off-latest-updates-cap-jap-sap-iim-abc-k-l)). Interviews then ran through February and March: IIM-C's WAT-PI across February 2026 ([Collegedunia IIM-C PI emails](https://collegedunia.com/news/iim-calcutta-begins-sending-pi-call-emails-for-mba-2026-28-alertid-145944)), IIM-B's in February–March ([Cracku IIM-B calls 2026](https://cracku.in/iim-bangalore-calls-2026/)), IIM-A's in February–March with results in April/May ([PW IIM admission 2026](https://www.pw.live/mba/exams/iim-admission-2026-and-pi-shortlist)), and CAP's from 23 February to 31 March with only three IIMs (Bodh Gaya, Sirmaur, Jammu) participating ([Careers360 CAP 2026](https://bschool.careers360.com/articles/iim-cap-registration)). Shifting those forward gives an **[ESTIMATE]** for 2027 of result ~20–31 December 2026, IIM-A/C shortlists ~7–10 January, IIM-B ~12–15 January, IIM-K ~15–18 January, interviews 1 February to end March with some IIMs into early April, and final results late April–May. The reading window between CAT and the first interview is therefore about nine to ten weeks, of which only three or four fall after the result; as one B-school guide warns, waiting for results is a common mistake because "only 4–6 weeks remain before interviews begin by then" ([Jaipuria preparing for WAT-PI](https://www.jaipuria.ac.in/blog/preparing-for-wat-pi-mba-admission-journey/)).

The WAT cadence is the one place the plan borrows time from reading. Guides converge on "one short essay every 2–3 days" with an introduction, body and conclusion ([Jaipuria WAT](https://www.jaipuria.ac.in/blog/cracking-the-written-ability-test-wat/)) and on practising 300-word pieces because "the more you practice 300 word articles, better your final 300 word actual WAT is going to be" ([2IIM WAT](https://online.2iim.com/cat-exam/blogs/cat-preparation-strategy/3-things-to-ace-wat-for-iim-interviews/)); IIM Lucknow's documented format is 15 minutes and 300 words ([Hitbullseye IIML WAT](https://gdpiportal.hitbullseye.com/iim-lucknow-wat.php)), CAP's was 20 minutes and 200–250 words ([Collegedunia CAP](https://collegedunia.com/exams/iim-cap)), and IIM-B and IIM-C report 25–30 minutes. A 15-minute timed essay plus 10 minutes of self-review on alternate days from late December yields roughly 25–30 practised essays before February, matching the 30–40-topic bank guidance, and the topics come free from your own diary headlines and the past-topic lists above. Write only claims you can defend orally, because the WAT frequently becomes the first PI thread.

The December block is also when the static material gets done, because transcripts show panels spending 10–12 minutes on work experience and then probing graduation fundamentals at definition level: a B.Com at IIM Nagpur was asked to define current ratio, net worth and return on sales ([Hitbullseye IIM Nagpur](https://gdpiportal.hitbullseye.com/iim-nagpur-gdpi-experiences.php)); a candidate who had studied water resources was asked about the water scenario in Marathwada ([CATKing CAP](https://catking.in/blog/iim-cap-personal-interview-experiences)); an IIM-A candidate from Qualcomm advises thinking about "how to explain your work to a common person" ([InsideIIM IIM-A 2021](https://insideiim.com/iim-ahmedabad-interview-experience-2021-vinayak-areguli)). None of that comes from the newspaper, and all of it is cheaper to prepare in December than in February. On books, no 2024–2026 convert source prescribes a canonical business-biography list; an IIM-A alumnus warns that "if you read to impress the panel, you'll be more concerned with completing the number of books than with gaining a deeper understanding" ([Careers360 alumnus Q&A](https://www.careers360.com/question-can-anyone-pls-suggest-some-books-to-be-mastered-in-iim-interview)), and the transcripts show panels asking for a favourite line, a plot summary and a link to Indian policy from whatever is named ([The Better India](https://thebetterindia.com/267106/iim-interview-questions-preparation-tips-iim-experiences-cat-quora)). One defendable book beats five skimmed ones.

## The 2026 you must be able to explain end-to-end

What follows is the briefing a candidate should be able to reproduce on the four-rung ladder: item, number, official position, own view. Dates are as reported; items flagged "single source" in the research should be re-verified against the primary release before being quoted to a panel.

### Indian economy: strong growth, imported inflation, weak rupee

India entered 2026 on a strong footing. Under the new 2022-23 base series released in February, FY26 real GDP growth printed **7.7% in the Provisional Estimates** (nominal 8.9%, secondary sector 8.8%, tertiary 9.3%, primary 3.2%) and was later revised to 7.8% ([PIB PE release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2269286&reg=48&lang=1); [Business Standard, 6 Oct 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/economy/news/world-bank-lifts-india-fy27-growth-view-to-7-1-on-robust-domestic-demand-126100601110_1.html)). **Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026) grew 7.8%** with manufacturing at 9.2% and investment demand (GFCF) at 11.9%, beating the RBI's 7.0% projection, and MoSPI began double-deflating manufacturing GVA ([MoSPI Q1 FY27 press note](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestReleases/latest_release_1788172583113_d65a77cf-240e-4491-82ee-59f78618fa41_Press_Note_on_GDP_Estimates_for_Q1_2026-27.pdf); [Business Standard, 31 Aug 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/economy/news/india-s-gdp-growth-accelerates-to-7-8-in-q1-fy27-despite-west-asia-shocks-126083100761_1.html)). The Economic Survey 2025-26 (29 January) projected FY27 at 6.8–7.2% and lifted potential growth to about 7% ([BusinessToday Economic Survey](https://www.businesstoday.in/union-budget/news/story/economic-survey-2026-live-fy27-gdp-growth-projected-at-68-72-all-eyes-on-union-budget-now-513435-2026-01-29)); the RBI (7 October) and the World Bank (6 October) now both put FY27 at **7.1%**. A caveat worth knowing: the IMF's April 2026 WEO put India's nominal GDP at ~$4.15 trillion, sixth behind Japan and the UK, because rupee depreciation shrank dollar GDP, so the 2025 "fourth-largest economy" claim rested on projections ([Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/business-news/latest-updates/india-no-longer-4th-largest-economy-drops-to-6th-place-in-imf-s-global-gdp-rankings/article-192243/)).

Then the oil shock arrived. US and Israeli operations against Iran began on **28 February 2026**; Iran declared Hormuz closed from 4 March; the Indian crude basket hit $113.57 by 11 March; India imports ~88% of its crude, ~51% of gas and ~60% of LPG, and holds only 5.33 million tonnes of strategic reserves, about nine to ten days ([IMPRI Hormuz](https://www.impriindia.com/insights/policy-update/india-strait-of-hormuz-crisis/); [CRS R45281](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R45281)). In late March the Centre cut special excise on petrol to Rs 3/litre from Rs 13 and on diesel to zero from Rs 10, holding pump prices, while imposing export duties on diesel and ATF ([CNBC, 27 Mar 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/27/india-fuel-tax-cut-oil-prices-iran-war-impact.html)); economists put the FY27 cost at ~Rs 1.5 trillion if sustained ([The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/india/excise-duty-cut-on-petrol-diesel-can-result-in-fiscal-loss-of-rs-1-5-trillion-economists-1503574884.html)), and OMCs raised petrol and diesel by ~Rs 3/litre on 15 May, the first hike in over four years ([Discovery Alert](https://discoveryalert.com/india-fuel-price-hike-strait-hormuz-crisis-2026/)). A second, domestic shock followed: the monsoon ended at 759 mm, roughly 12.6–13% below the long-period average and the weakest in over a decade on a strong El Niño, with Maharashtra declaring drought in 265 of 358 tehsils ([TechTimes, 30 Sep 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/328334/20260930/india-monsoon-2026-ends-worst-deficit-over-decade-imd-confirms.htm)).

The result is the paradox a panel will want you to resolve. CPI under the new 2024=100 series printed 2.75% in January; August came in at **4.82%**, the highest since December 2024, with food and beverages at 5.52%, and a Reuters poll expects September (due 12 October) at 5.40%, a fourth straight month above the 4% target ([ICRA](https://www.icra.in/Research/ViewResearchReport/7058); [Business Standard, 8 Oct 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/economy/news/india-inflation-likely-rose-to-5-4-in-sep-on-food-energy-costs-poll-126100800164_1.html)). After holding at 5.25% in April, June and August, the RBI's 63rd MPC on **7 October 2026 raised the repo rate 25 bps to 5.50%**, unanimous on the rate, and shifted the stance from neutral to "calibrated tightening" with two members (Nagesh Kumar, Ram Singh) preferring neutral; SDF is 5.25% and MSF/Bank Rate 5.75%; the MPC projects FY27 CPI at 5.2% (Q3 6.0%) and growth at 7.1% ([SCC Online RBI hike](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/10/09/rbi-hikes-repo-rate-to-5-50-percent-october-2026/); [Business Standard, 7 Oct 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/finance/news/rbi-mpc-raises-fy27-inflation-forecast-to-5-2-projects-gdp-growth-at-7-1-126100700283_1.html)). The rupee has hit a record low near **Rs 96.96/$** as Brent crossed $102 ([Trading Economics rupee](https://tradingeconomics.com/india/currency/news/538123)), and forex reserves fell a **record $18.34 billion in the week to 25 September to $747.56 billion** as the RBI sold dollars ([Bloomberg, 2 Oct 2026](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-02/india-forex-reserves-log-record-weekly-drop-on-rupee-defense)). The Q1 FY27 current-account deficit widened to $4.2 billion (0.5% of GDP) on an $86.1 billion merchandise deficit, cushioned by $51.6 billion of net services and $42.9 billion of remittances, with an overall BoP deficit of $8.1 billion ([Business Standard CAD](https://www.business-standard.com/economy/news/india-s-current-account-deficit-widens-to-4-2-bn-in-q1-as-trade-gap-grows-126090101448_1.html)). The interview debate is whether a rate hike can address supply-driven inflation and whether "calibrated tightening" is really about the currency; the honest answer connects the hike to inflation credibility and to the cost of defending the rupee through reserves alone.

Fiscal and tax policy give the second cluster of numbers. Budget 2026-27, presented on **1 February 2026** (a Sunday; Sitharaman's ninth consecutive), set capex at **Rs 12.2 lakh crore**, effective capex at Rs 17.15 lakh crore, total expenditure at ~Rs 53.5 lakh crore and the **fiscal deficit at 4.3% of GDP** (from 4.4% in FY26 RE), with debt-to-GDP at 55.6% on a path toward ~50% by 2030-31 ([KPMG Budget 2026-27](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/in/pdf/2026/02/public-finance-pov-union-budget-2026-27.pdf); [Continuum Economics](https://continuumeconomics.com/a/361be221/indias-union-fy27-budget-a-pragmatic-blueprint-that-prioritises-stability-over-stimulus)). Income-tax slabs were unchanged; STT was raised (futures 0.02% to 0.05%, options 0.15%), buybacks became taxable as capital gains for all shareholders, customs duty was exempted on 17 cancer drugs, and a tax holiday to 2047 was offered to foreign cloud providers operating from Indian data centres ([Insights on India Budget](https://www.insightsonindia.com/2026/02/01/union-budget-2026-27-highlights-upsc/)). The **Income-tax Act 2025 came into force on 1 April 2026**, replacing the 1961 Act, cutting sections from 819 to 536 and introducing "Tax Year" ([PIB, 1 Apr 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2248005&reg=48&lang=2)). GST 2.0's two-slab structure from 22 September 2025 has not dented revenue: September 2026 gross GST was **Rs 2.04 lakh crore, up 14.7%**, the third straight month above Rs 2 lakh crore, though domestic GST grew a cleaner 10.1% with import GST inflated by oil and the rupee ([CAclubindia GST Sept 2026](https://www.caclubindia.com/news/gst-collections-rise-14-7-to-rs-2-04-lakh-crore-in-september-2026-26828.asp)). By August the fiscal deficit had reached 41.9% of the full-year target, versus 38.1% a year earlier ([Business Standard, 30 Sep 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/economy/news/centre-s-fiscal-deficit-touches-41-9-of-full-year-target-at-aug-end-cga-126093000917_1.html)). Key officials: RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra, Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman (who also chairs the GST Council), CEA V. Anantha Nageswaran, SEBI Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey, Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal.

Trade is the third cluster and the messiest. A US–India interim framework announced on 6 February 2026 cut the US tariff on most Indian goods to 18% and removed the 25% Russian-oil penalty, with India pledging over $500 billion of US purchases ([White House joint statement](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/02/united-states-india-joint-statement/); [India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/us-india-interim-trade-agreement-18-percent-tariff-42514.html/)). But on **20 February 2026 the US Supreme Court held in Learning Resources v. Trump that IEEPA does not authorise tariffs** ([Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/02/supreme-court-strikes-down-ieepa-tariffs)); a 10% Section 122 surcharge ran until 24 July and was replaced by Section 301 duties ([ABA](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/public_contract_law/resources/procurement-lawyer/2026-fall/ieepa-tariffs-struck-down-replacement-tariffs-expired/)); and on 18 September Trump signed the Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026, authorising up to 100% tariffs on the five largest buyers of Russian crude, which includes India, whose Russian share of crude imports rose to 48.6% in June ([Tribune sanctions bill](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/how-india-and-its-oil-economy-would-be-impacted-as-us-approves-sanctions-bill-on-russia); [ChemAnalyst](https://www.chemanalyst.com/NewsAndDeals/NewsDetails/russian-crude-nears-50-of-indias-imports-will-us-tariffs-44556)). USTR Greer said on 30 September a deal is "not imminent" ([Archyde](https://www.archyde.com/us-and-india-stall-on-washington-trade-talks-over-russian-oil-purchases/)). If asked "what is the US tariff on India today?", the defensible answer is that it is contested and must be checked with USTR, and you should explain why. The diversification response is concrete: the **India–UK CETA entered into force on 15 July 2026** with zero duty on ~99% of Indian export lines ([UK Parliament statement](https://questions-statements.parliament.uk/written-statements/detail/2026-07-15/hlws270)), and the **India–EU FTA concluded on 27 January 2026** after about 20 years, with signing expected 16 December 2026 and entry into force in early 2027 ([Cyril Amarchand](https://corporate.cyrilamarchandblogs.com/2026/09/the-india-eu-free-trade-agreement-a-landmark-deal-moves-toward-finalisation/)).

Corporate India supplies the "sector tie-in" stories. Jio Platforms' ~Rs 37,700 crore IPO, India's largest ever, received SEBI's observation letter on 28 August with a tentative 21–23 October subscription and 28 October listing (unofficial), enabled by SEBI's cut in the minimum public offer to 2.5% for issuers above Rs 5 lakh crore ([Outlook Money Jio IPO](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/jio-platforms-ipo-launch-date-issue-size-price-band-listing-date); [India Infoline](https://www.indiainfoline.com/news/companies/jio-platforms-ipo-2026-33000-crore-issue-october-21-opening-date-gmp-rises-to-160)). A Tata Sons board approved N. Chandrasekaran's reappointment 4–1 in late September with Tata Trusts chairman Noel Tata dissenting and the Trusts (holding ~66%) calling the resolution a "legal nullity"; his current term ends 20 February 2027 ([Outlook Business Tata](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/tata-sons-vs-noel-tata-voting-math-behind-boardroom-battle-explained)). Cabinet approved **India Semiconductor Mission 2.0 on 30 June 2026 with ~Rs 1.25 lakh crore** ([Business Standard ISM 2.0](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/finance/news/govt-approves-1-25-trillion-outlay-for-india-semiconductor-mission-2-0-126063000569_1.html)); Micron's Sanand assembly-test plant opened 28 February ([Micron press release](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2026/Micron-Celebrates-Opening-of-Indias-First-Semiconductor-Assembly-and-Test-Facility-02-28-2026/default.aspx)), while Tata's Dholera fab slipped to mid-2028 commercial output at 90 nm ([TechTimes, 17 Jul 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320824/20260717/indias-first-chip-fab-opens-90nm-two-process-nodes-below-what-tata-promised.htm)), a distinction between ATMP and fab a panel will test. Banks are at a multi-decade low GNPA of 1.8% with CRAR 17.7% per the June FSR, but credit growth of ~18.6–19% against deposit growth near 11.5% is a live funding-gap theme ([RMA India FSR June 2026](https://rmaindia.org/reserve-bank-of-india-rbi-releases-financial-stability-report-june-2026/); [ANI credit growth](https://aninews.in/news/business/indias-bank-credit-growth-remains-robust-at-186-in-june-2026-nbfc-retail-lending-drive-momentum-report20260803101315/)). Defence exports hit a record Rs 38,424 crore in FY26, up 62.66% ([Business Standard defence exports](https://www.business-standard.com/blueprint-defence-magazine/reports/india-s-defence-exports-hit-record-rs-38-424-crore-in-fy26-up-62-66-126040200445_1.html)); renewables added a record ~51 GW in FY26 with cumulative capacity at 299.25 GW by 30 September ([Saur Energy](https://www.saurenergy.com/solar-energy-news/india-adds-2456-gw-renewable-energy-capacity-in-h1-fy-2026-27-12637483)); EV sales crossed 24.5 lakh in FY26 ([Autocar Pro](https://www.autocarpro.in/analysis-sales/ev-sales-in-india-surpass-245-million-units-in-fy2026-all-4-segments-register-double-digit-growth-131974)). ### Global: the Iran war reversed the easing cycle and reshuffled the neighbourhood

The Iran war is the one story that explains everything else. The 28 February strikes killed Supreme Leader Khamenei; Hormuz, through which ~25% of seaborne oil passed, saw tanker transits fall near zero; Pakistan brokered a two-week ceasefire on 8 April and hosted the Islamabad Talks; a June memorandum collapsed in July when Iran attacked commercial vessels; and on 8–9 October the Pentagon told CENTCOM to prepare for a possible resumption of major combat operations with Brent at ~$103–105 ([Wikipedia 2026 Iran war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war); [Al Jazeera live, 9 Oct 2026](https://aljazeera.com/amp/news/liveblog/2026/10/9/iran-war-live-iranian-media-reports-massive-explosions-in-hormuz-strait); [Euronews, 8 Oct 2026](https://euronews.com/2026/10/08/oil-prices-are-climbing-on-concerns-about-an-escalating-iran-war)). India's exposure is direct: ~half of crude and over three-quarters of LPG imports transit Hormuz ([EPIC India](https://epic.uchicago.in/how-us-israel-iran-war-is-hitting-indias-oil-supply/)); over 52,000 Indians were evacuated from the Gulf in the first week of March ([Takshashila](https://takshashila.org.in/content/publications/assets/Indias-Response-War-150526.pdf)); and roughly $51 billion of India's $135 billion remittances come from the Gulf ([CNBC, 5 Mar 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/05/iran-conflict-india-impact-remittance-pipeline.html)). India's response was studied neutrality: evacuations, a US waiver for Russian cargoes, Venezuelan imports, and a BRICS New Delhi Declaration that called for "restraint" without naming either side ([Al Jazeera BRICS, 12 Sep 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/12/brics-summit-opens-in-new-delhi-amid-wars-global-tensions-tariffs)). The war also suspended US-brokered Russia–Ukraine talks from February; both sides said in late September they are ready to resume trilateral talks, possibly in the UAE ([Al Jazeera, 4 Oct 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/4/ukraine-ready-for-us-backed-talks-with-russia-zelenskyy)).

Monetary policy turned globally. The Fed under Chair Kevin Warsh raised rates 25 bps to 3.75–4.00% on 16 September 2026, its first hike since 2023, unanimous, with one more pencilled in for 2026 ([CNBC Fed, 16 Sep 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/fed-rate-decision-september-2026.html)); the IMF's April WEO put 2026 global growth at 3.1% and inflation at 4.4% with energy prices up 19% ([IMF WEO April 2026](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/weo/2026/april/english/ch1.pdf)); gold peaked at ~$5,589 on 28 January and Bitcoin fell ~54% from its October 2025 peak to about $80,000 ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/bitcoin-correlation-gold-just-hit-153154868.html)). The US–China truce was extended only to 10 January 2027 at the September Trump–Xi summit, with a "Super Intelligence Dialogue" on AI risk ([CNBC, 25 Sep 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/25/trump-xi-summit-takeaways.html)). On H-1B, selection became wage-weighted from 27 February 2026, a $100,000 fee applies to overseas hires, and Indian consulates show no stamping slots for the rest of 2026 ([News on AIR H-1B](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/us-h-1b-visa-selection-shifts-from-lottery-to-wage-based-system); [M9 News](https://www.m9.news/usa-news/h1b-visa-slots-2026-indians-stranded-india/)).

India's neighbourhood reset in a single quarter: BNP's Tarique Rahman won 209 of 299 seats in Bangladesh on 12 February with Jamaat-e-Islami as main opposition ([Al Jazeera Bangladesh](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/2/12/live-results-bangladesh-election-2026)); Balen Shah's RSP won 182 of 275 in Nepal on 5 March, the first single-party majority in its democratic history ([UK Commons Library](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10622/)); Myanmar's junta installed Min Aung Hlaing as president on 10 April after an election ASEAN refused to endorse ([The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2026/02/military-proxy-party-declared-lopsided-victor-in-sham-myanmar-election/)); and Carney's 27 February–3 March visit to Delhi produced a C$2.6 billion uranium deal and launched CEPA talks ([Bloomberg, 2 Mar 2026](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-02/carney-meets-modi-in-new-delhi-marking-new-chapter-in-ties)). India–China is in a tactical thaw (Wang Yi–Doval talks in June, boundary talks 25 August) over a structural ~$100 billion deficit ([GlobalSecurity Wang Yi](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/china/2026/06/china-260623-globaltimes01.htm); [Britannica India–China](https://www.britannica.com/topic/India-China-relations)); India–Pakistan remains frozen a year after Operation Sindoor with the Indus Waters Treaty in abeyance and only an unofficial Track-II meeting in Colombo ([Outlook Track-II](https://www.outlookindia.com/international/outlook-explains-whats-driving-india-pakistans-track-ii-talks-after-operation-sindoor)). ### Social and political: delimitation, caste census, labour codes, five new state governments

The most charged domestic issue going into 2027 is delimitation. ### Twelve opinion questions to have a stance on

Because panels end on "what is your view", each issue page should close with a position and its strongest counter. The cross-cutting bank, each with its two sides drawn from the facts above: was the RBI right to hike to 5.50% into an oil shock (credibility and rupee defence versus supply-driven inflation and 7.1% growth); should India cut Russian crude to near zero to avoid a 100% US tariff (48.6% import share and Hormuz risk versus the sanctions law and the unsigned deal); was the February interim deal a win or a capitulation (18% from 50% versus the $500 billion pledge and 126% solar CVD); is AI a threat to Indian IT (TCS cuts versus 6.1% growth and GCCs); should India build frontier models or applications (Sarvam 105B versus the GPU gap); is delimitation on the 2011 census fair to the South (women's reservation versus 23.87%); will a caste census deepen or reduce inequality; is BRICS useful if it cannot name the US; should India have condemned the strike on Iran (7,000 Indians in Iran and Global South leadership versus the US deal and energy diplomacy); should gig workers be employees; should India ban social media for under-16s; and should India spend on a 2036 Olympics. Take one side on each, because the IIM-L advice is explicit that "it is crucial to not only look at both the sides but also choose one" ([InsideIIM 3-step](https://insideiim.com/get-iim-tips-iim-lucknow-student)).

## Conclusion

The research changes the question from "what should I read" to "what can I defend four follow-ups deep". Panels in 2024–2026 did not reward breadth; they rewarded a candidate who could name a business story, give its number, state the official position and hold a view under challenge, and they punished a confidently wrong fact more than an honest "I don't know". That makes the 30-minute constraint less of a handicap than it looks, because the routine that fits it, two articles read properly and one issue page kept current, is the same routine the converts describe for an hour. The real risk is not reading too little but reading the wrong things: headline apps, long-form paid subscriptions and UPSC magazines all fail the depth test that the sovereign-bonds and DCGI exchanges illustrate.

Two implications follow for this specific cycle. First, the 2026 story is unusually coherent, so a candidate who can narrate the chain from Hormuz to the 7 October hike, and then to the FTAs, the rupee, the markets and the Budget 2027-28 trade-offs, has a single spine on which almost every economic follow-up can hang. Second, the calendar front-loads the hardest updates into the interview window itself: the First Advance Estimates, Economic Survey, Budget and February MPC will all land after the shortlists arrive, which means the plan's success depends less on October's reading than on having a numbers sheet and three or four issue pages ready to be rebuilt in a single sitting in the first week of February 2027.
